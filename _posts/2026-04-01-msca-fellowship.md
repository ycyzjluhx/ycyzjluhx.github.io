I started my Horizon Europe **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowship** at University College London. The project, **Spatial Multiplexing Fluid Antenna Systems (SM-FAS)**, investigates spatial multiplexing architectures, analytical foundations, algorithms, and prototype validation for fluid antenna systems in 6G wireless communications.
