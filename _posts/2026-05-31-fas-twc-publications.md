date: 2026-05-31 09:00:00 +0100

Two first-author papers on fluid antenna systems appeared in *IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications* in 2026. One develops a geometric approach to error probability and fundamental limits of FAS, while the other studies **FAS-enabled UAV communications in the finite-blocklength regime**. Together, they connect FAS theory with reliability, mobility, and short-packet transmission.
