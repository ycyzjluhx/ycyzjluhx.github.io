# Research

<p class="lede">My research develops reconfigurable wireless technologies for 6G, combining communication theory, transceiver design, performance analysis, optimization, and emerging hardware architectures.</p>

## Fluid and Movable Antenna Systems

I study **fluid antenna systems (FAS)** and movable antenna architectures that exploit spatial reconfigurability at the antenna level. Current topics include fundamental error-performance limits, port selection and combining, spatial multiplexing, finite-blocklength transmission, UAV communications, switching constraints, and prototype-aware system design. A longer-term goal is to connect analytical models with measurement-driven and hardware validation.

## Reconfigurable Intelligent / Fluid Surfaces

My work on **reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS)** and **fluid reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (FRIS)** covers reflective and transmissive architectures, secure transmission, beamforming and pattern co-design, and programmable propagation. I am particularly interested in moving beyond fixed passive surfaces toward surfaces and antenna structures whose electromagnetic responses can be jointly reconfigured with the communication waveform.

## Spatial and Index Modulation

I have worked extensively on **space shift keying, spatial modulation, spatial scattering modulation, and related index-modulation techniques**. This research includes detector design, exact and asymptotic error analysis, discrete-phase optimization, and transmission over mmWave sparse channels. The central theme is to encode information in controllable spatial states while maintaining low radio-frequency hardware complexity.

## AI-Assisted Reconfigurable Wireless Systems

I am extending this research toward **AI-assisted reconfigurable wireless systems**, including learning-based port and surface control, model-driven learning, graph neural networks, reinforcement learning, and autonomous configuration. The objective is to combine the interpretability and guarantees of communication theory with data-driven adaptation for dynamic 6G environments.

## Related Topics

Related interests include **mmWave MIMO, finite-blocklength and URLLC, UAV and low-altitude communications, integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), channel measurement, physical-layer security, transceiver optimization, and performance analysis**.
